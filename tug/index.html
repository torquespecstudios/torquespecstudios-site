How to play

Steering

Hold the left or right side of the screen, or ← → (A D). Boats keep their momentum, and barges swing wide on bends, so start turns early.

Racing

Get down the river fast without wrecking the hull or the cargo. Fuel cans mark the safe channel. Rapids push you sideways; sandbars slow you down.

Horn and tow line

Horn (B) makes fishing boats ahead give way. Tugs can heave the tow line in or pay it out (▲ ▼, or Q E): shorter swings less but yanks harder.

Jobs

You drive the throttle (lever on the right, or W S). Slow down, put a barge on the hip (H), come alongside the amber berth under 1.5 kn, and Make fast (M).

Fuel transfer

Swing the hose crane over the manifold and bolt up, crank the generator and close the breaker, set the pump direction, open all three valves, start the pump and bring the throttle up slowly. Watch the pressure (red line 150 psi) and the tank. Then shut down in reverse and stow the hose.

Crane work

Slew, boom and hoist (A D, W S, ↑ ↓) to put the hook over a load, lock on (Space), lift, swing it over its pad or bay, turn containers to line up (Q E), and set it down gently. The load swings if you slew hard, and the limiter stops you booming out too far.
