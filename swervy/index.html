The game

Tap to steer. Let go to swing.

Swervy is a one-tap racing game on an endless mountain road. There's one control: tap to steer, let go to swing back. The road keeps curving and the hairpins keep tightening, so every run is a test of how far you can drive before you run out of road. It's built for iPhone and iPad, and the beta plays right in your phone's browser.

How to play

One finger, endless road

Tap anywhere to steer left, and hold a little longer to turn harder. Let go and the car swings right. Stay on the road, grab fuel cans for points, and chain them quickly for up to a ×3 combo. Skim the edge and recover for a close-call bonus.

Drive far enough and the road climbs from a golden-hour pine forest into Autumn Ridge, where fallen logs and rockfalls block the edges, and on to Frostline Pass, with snow and black ice at night.

Features

  • One-tap controls anyone can pick up in seconds
  • Eight cars to unlock, upgrade, paint and style
  • A Daily Road that's the same for everyone, every day
  • Missions, driver levels, trophies and a daily reward streak
  • Free to play, no account, and it plays offline

Questions

Swervy FAQ

Is Swervy free?

Yes. Swervy is free to play, and everything in the game can be earned by driving.

How do I play the Swervy beta?

Open torquespecstudios.com/play on your phone. There's nothing to download. On iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, to play full screen like an app. Turn Silent Mode off to hear the engine.

When is Swervy coming to the App Store?

Swervy is coming soon to the Apple App Store for iPhone and iPad. Follow @playswervy on Instagram for the launch date.

Can I compete with my friends?

Yes. Pick Daily Road on the start screen: it's the exact same road for everyone that day, so you can compare distances. Tap Share after a run to send your score card.

Who makes Swervy?

Swervy is made by Torque Spec Studios, an independent game and app studio in Washington State.
