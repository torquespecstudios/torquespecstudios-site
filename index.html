SwervyOne-Tap Racer · iPhone, iPad and Android

TuggyRiver work boats · plays in your browser

Run the river in a tug and its barge, then take on the real work. Push a cargo ship alongside the wharf, run a car ferry across the current, dig a channel with a clamshell crane, pump fuel at the refinery, and find capsized paddlers in the fog.

  • 14 boats: tugs towing one, two or three barges, a towboat, a crane barge, a car ferry, a sternwheeler, a fireboat, a pilot boat and a water scooter
  • 12 hands-on jobs: valves, generator and pump on the fuel barge, a crane with a load-moment limiter, ferry ramps and gates, and ship assist with a tug that rolls coal when it pushes
  • Four rivers, from rapids and a foggy bend to a harbor mouth with cargo ships, plus night runs
  • Gold times, upgrades, paint jobs, trophies and daily missions

ScoopyOne-tap excavator · plays in your browser

Drive an excavator up the job site. Tap left or right to swing between lanes, scoop the dirt piles, and pull up beside the haul trucks to dump your load. Dodge the cones, survey stakes, open trenches and the foreman's pickup, and never dig where the spray paint marks a buried line.

About the studio

Games and apps built to spec

Torque Spec Studios is an independent game and app studio based in Washington State. The name comes from the shop floor: a torque spec is the exact amount of force a bolt has to be tightened to. Not close, not good enough, exactly right. That's the standard we hold every game and app to.

Swervy is the first. Swoopy Wings, a low-flying air racer, Tuggy, a river work-boat game, and Scoopy, a one-tap excavator game, are in open beta, and more games and apps are on the way.
