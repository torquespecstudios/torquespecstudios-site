Privacy policy

Effective October 23, 2026 · Applies to these apps for iPhone, iPad and Android: Swervy: One-Tap Racer, Scoopy, Swoopy Wings and Tuggy

What we collect

Nothing. None of our games has a sign-in, and none of them makes network requests: graphics, sounds and fonts are all built into the app, and every game works in airplane mode. We cannot see who plays, how they play, or where they are.

What is saved on your device

To keep your progress, each game saves a small game file on your phone or tablet. Depending on the game it contains:

  • best times, distances, scores and medals;
  • fuel cans, and the cars, machines, planes or boats, paint and upgrades you've unlocked;
  • level, missions, trophies and daily streak;
  • ghost runs of your best races, so you can race against yourself (Swoopy Wings and Tuggy);
  • settings such as sound, vibration, units and graphics quality.

The file stays on your device, and a copy is kept in the app's own storage so updates don't erase your progress. Deleting the app deletes both. If your device backs up apps (iCloud Backup on iPhone and iPad, or Google's backup on Android), that backup may include the file, under Apple's or Google's own privacy terms. We never receive it.

Sharing your score

The Share button opens your device's standard share sheet with a score card picture and a short message. Nothing is sent unless you pick an app and send it yourself. We never receive what you share.

Ratings

Our games may occasionally show the built-in rating prompt: Apple's "Enjoying…?" prompt on iPhone and iPad, or Google Play's in-app review card on Android. Apple and Google handle these entirely; we see only the public ratings and reviews in the stores.

Voice callouts (Swoopy Wings)

Swoopy Wings can speak short warnings such as "Pull up". The speech is made by your device's own text-to-speech voice. You can turn callouts off in Settings.

Feedback email

If you email us, from a game's "Send feedback" link or directly, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply and to improve the games, and we delete it on request.

Children

Our games are made for all ages. Because they collect no personal information from anyone, they collect none from children under 13.

Third parties

The current versions of our games contain no advertising, analytics or tracking companies, and no data is sold or shared.

This website

torquespecstudios.com uses GoatCounter to count page visits. It uses no cookies and collects no personal information, only anonymous totals such as which pages were viewed, the referring site, browser and screen size. This applies to the website and the free browser betas only, not to the apps.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of any game adds anything that handles data, such as optional rewarded ads, invite-a-friend rewards or online leaderboards, this policy and the store privacy labels will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.

Questions about privacy: torquespecstudios@gmail.com

Support

How to play, common questions and how to reach us

Swervy: One-Tap Racer

Touch and hold anywhere to steer left (hold longer to turn harder), and let go to swing right. Stay on the road, grab fuel cans for points, and chain them within 2.5 seconds for up to an x3 combo. Skim the edge and recover for a close-call bonus. Spend cans in the garage on new cars, paint and upgrades. To see the tutorial again, open Settings in the garage and tap "Replay the tutorial on next drive".

Scoopy

Tap the left or right side of the screen to swing one lane over. Drive over dirt piles to scoop them, then sit in the lane next to a waiting haul truck to dump into it. Load the job's goal of trucks for a medal. Cones, survey stakes, trenches and the foreman's pickup wreck you, and hitting a spray-painted utility line always ends the job. Diesel cans refill the tank, a hard hat takes one hit, and coffee is a short boost. "How to play" in the yard explains it all again.

Swoopy Wings

Drag left or right to bank and turn, and up or down to climb and dive. Slide the throttle down for tighter turns. Fly through the gold rings for speed and points, stay low for bonus points, and watch out for canyon walls, towers, bridges and birds. To take the flying lesson again, open Settings in the hangar and tap "Replay the flying lesson".

Tuggy

Hold the left or right side of the screen to steer. Boats keep their momentum and barges swing wide on bends, so start turns early. In races, fuel cans mark the safe channel. In jobs you also drive the throttle lever: slow down, come alongside the amber berth gently and make fast, then follow the banksman's cues for crane and hose work. "How to play" in the garage explains every job.

I lost my progress. Can it be restored?

Progress is stored only on your device. Updating a game keeps it, but deleting the game removes it. Restoring the device from a backup made before the deletion (iCloud or computer on iPhone and iPad, Google backup on Android) brings it back.

Can I move my progress to a new phone?

Yes, by setting up the new phone from a backup of the old one. Progress can't move between an iPhone and an Android phone, because each game keeps it only on the device.

How do I turn the sound or vibration off?

Every game has a gear icon on its main screen. Sound, music, vibration, graphics quality and more are there.

Do the games have ads or in-app purchases?

Not right now: everything is earned by playing. A future update may add optional ads you choose to watch for a reward, and a one-time purchase to remove them. This page and the store listings will say so first.

A game feels slow on my phone.

Our games lower their own resolution when a phone struggles. You can also set Graphics to Low in Settings, which saves battery too. Closing other apps and turning off Low Power Mode or Battery Saver both help.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Every game works fully offline.
