How to play

Swinging lanes

Tap the left side of the screen to swing one lane left, and the right side to swing one lane right (or use ← → or A D).

Scooping

Drive over dirt piles and the bucket scoops them by itself. Piles in a line build a combo up to x5. The bucket meter is bottom left: with a full bucket, another pile spills, costs 100 points and breaks the combo.

Loading trucks

A haul truck backs up on the left or right haul road. Sit in the lane next to it and you swing over and dump automatically. Six scoops fill a truck: +500 and fuel cans. Trucks only wait so long.

Hazards

Cones, survey stakes, open trenches and the foreman's pickup wreck you. Spray-painted marks are buried utility lines: hitting one ends the job, even with a hard hat.

Pickups

Diesel cans refill the tank (empty means game over). A hard hat takes one hit for you. Coffee is a short speed boost with double points.

Jobs and machines

Load the job's goal of trucks in one run for bronze, two more for silver, four more for gold. Finishing a job opens the next one and unlocks a machine. Spend fuel cans on machines, upgrades and revives.
